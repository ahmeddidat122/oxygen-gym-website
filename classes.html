Classes & Schedule

From high-intensity HIIT to restorative yoga — find your perfect class and instructor.

What We Offer

Class Descriptions

Intermediate 45 min

High-intensity intervals designed to torch calories and boost cardiovascular fitness. Expect circuits, bodyweight moves, and short rest periods.

  • Full-body conditioning
  • Modifications available for all levels
  • Bring a towel and water bottle
Beginner 60 min

Flow-based yoga blending strength, flexibility, and breathwork. Ideal for stress relief and improving mobility after heavy lifting days.

  • All experience levels welcome
  • Mats provided — wear comfortable clothing
  • Great complement to strength training
Beginner – Intermediate 50 min

Learn proper form for squats, deadlifts, presses, and rows. Build confidence with barbells and dumbbells in a coached group setting.

  • Technique-focused coaching
  • Progressive programming each week
  • Perfect for new lifters
All Levels 45 min

Indoor cycling combined with targeted core work. Ride to the beat, then finish with abs and stability exercises.

  • Adjustable bike resistance
  • High-energy music and coaching
  • Low impact on joints
Advanced 60 min

Outdoor-style training indoors. Sled pushes, battle ropes, kettlebells, and team challenges. Not for the faint of heart.

  • Maximum calorie burn
  • Team atmosphere and accountability
  • Arrive 10 minutes early to warm up
