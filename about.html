About Oxygen Gym

A welcoming community where beginners and experienced lifters train side by side.

Our Story

More Than a Gym

Oxygen Gym was founded in Bogura with one belief: fitness should feel empowering, not intimidating. Whether you're walking in for the first time or chasing a new personal record, you'll find certified coaches, spotless facilities, and a community that cheers for every rep. We're not just building stronger bodies — we're building a stronger Bogura.
